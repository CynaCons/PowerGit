Review mode in the Diff panel

Clickable draft. Turn review mode on with the round button at the bottom of the file list, next to the tree toggle. Every changed or added line then starts as unreviewed; click a line to mark it reviewed, click again to undo.

↑ ↓ or k j move the cursor a line at a time, gg and G jump to the top and bottom, Space toggles the current line, n jumps to the next unreviewed line, Enter goes to the next file that still has work. In the artifact viewer the first click only focuses the page; click again.

    What this drafts, and what comes after

    1. Review state is per commit (or per working-directory snapshot) and per line, keyed by file, side and line number, so it survives switching files and reopening the app until the diff itself changes.
    2. Context lines are never part of the count. Only + and − lines are, because that is what a reviewer is accountable for.
    3. The hover “+” on a changed line is the next step: a note attached to that line, kept as a draft, and sent together to the AI reviewer when you press Finish review, the way a Bitbucket review collects comments before submit.
